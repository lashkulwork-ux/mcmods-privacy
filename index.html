Privacy Policy

Applies to all mobile applications published by Artem Lashkul, including MC Bedrock Mods (com.mcmodshub.app), MC Java Mods (com.mcmodshub.jav) and PixWall (com.pixwall.app).

Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

We respect your privacy. Our apps are content browsers that let you discover and download community-made Minecraft mods, add-ons, maps, textures and similar content. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or precise location. This policy explains what limited data is processed when you use the app and which third-party services are involved.

2. Information We Process

Data stored only on your device

  • Your favorites, download history and recently downloaded files.
  • Your in-app preferences (filters, selected language, consent choices).
  • Whether you purchased the ad-free option.

This information stays on your device (and in your Google account backup, if you have Android Auto Backup enabled). We do not upload it to our own servers — we operate no servers that store personal data.

Data processed by third-party SDKs

  • Advertising identifiers and device/usage signals, collected by our advertising and analytics providers (see Section 3) to show ads and measure app performance.
  • Approximate, coarse signals such as country/region, device model, OS version, app version and crash diagnostics.
  • Install attribution (e.g. the campaign that led you to install) via the Google Play Install Referrer.

3. Third-Party Services

Our apps use the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — displays ads; may use your advertising ID and device data for ad delivery and (with consent) personalization. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Ads — additional ad network used through AdMob mediation. Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous, aggregated usage statistics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and stability diagnostics.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — app start and rendering performance metrics.
  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing — core platform services and the processing of in-app purchases (see Section 5).

4. Ads & Your Consent

Our apps are free and supported by ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we show a Google-certified consent screen (Google User Messaging Platform) on first launch where you can accept or decline personalized ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can also remove ads entirely via an optional in-app purchase.

5. In-App Purchases

Some apps offer optional purchases (for example, "Remove Ads", a temporary ad-free boost, or voluntary "tip" donations). All payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your card number or payment details — Google handles the transaction and only tells our app whether a purchase succeeded so we can unlock the corresponding feature.

6. Downloaded Content

Mods and other content are hosted by third parties (such as CurseForge and Modrinth). When you download an item, your device connects directly to those services, which may process your IP address as part of delivering the file. We do not control those services' data practices.

7. Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

8. Data Retention & Your Choices

  • On-device data is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.
  • Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR / CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete data held by the third-party providers listed above; please use their respective tools and policies, or contact us for help.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
